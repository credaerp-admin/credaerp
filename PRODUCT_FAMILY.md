# Creda Product Family

CredaERP is the shared platform. Customer-facing products are vertical products built on the same codebase:

| Product | Primary audience | Default vertical |
|---|---|---|
| CredaRx | Indian pharmacies and medical stores | Pharmacy |
| CredaRetail | General retail stores | General retail |

Use product-specific landing pages and sales collateral, while keeping accounting, GST, inventory, backup, training, and support foundations shared.

## Sales message: CredaRx

**Positioning:** Fast, reliable pharmacy billing and inventory control for Indian medical stores.

**Ideal customer:** Independent pharmacies and small chains with one to three counters moving beyond Excel, manual registers, Marg, or difficult-to-train software.

**Demo flow:** Set up catalog → bill a medicine → record a cash/credit split → show batch and expiry → review customer credit → close the day → export GST/reporting output.

**Proof to collect:** onboarding time, migrated item count, billing speed, stock-loss reduction, expiry recovery, and time saved during GST preparation. Do not publish testimonials or quantified savings without customer approval and evidence.

## Campaign attribution

Use URLs such as `?product=credarx&source=whatsapp&campaign=pharmacy-demo` and store these parameters with every demo request. Report lead, demo, trial, activation, paid conversion, and churn by product.
