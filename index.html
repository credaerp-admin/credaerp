Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 3-month free trial work?
You get full, unrestricted access to CredaERP for 3 months completely free. We do not ask for any credit card or security deposits. It's a completely risk-free way to verify how our billing, inventory, and compliance modules benefit your business operations.
How is the software activated?
CredaERP includes an offline-first architecture for maximum billing terminal reliability. When you register for the trial, we generate a license key tied securely to your machine's hardware ID. You input this key during setup, and the application activates instantly—working 100% offline without needing a constant internet connection.
Does CredaERP include a mobile app?
Yes. The mobile companion app pairs with the desktop POS over your local network for barcode scanning, product lookup, stock checks and cart mirroring. Request the mobile setup with your demo and our onboarding team will help you pair it securely.
What is the Premium Business Chat Assistant?
It is a Premium-only business assistant for quick operational questions such as monthly sales, available stock, medicine location, fast-moving medicines, customer or supplier balances and reorder suggestions. You can type English, Hindi pronunciation or Bengali pronunciation using English letters. It is designed for fast, focused answers from your POS data and is available on desktop and paired mobile.
Can I migrate my existing inventory from Excel or another software?
Yes! We provide built-in CSV Excel import templates that allow you to import your entire medicine catalog, HSN mappings, and opening stock in a single click. Our support team is also available to help you import your existing data.
Can I use my existing thermal printer/barcode scanner?
Yes, CredaERP supports all standard USB and network thermal receipt printers (2-inch or 3-inch sizes) as well as regular A4/A5 laser printers. Any standard plug-and-play barcode scanner works instantly without any drivers.
What happens after my 3-month trial ends?
When the trial expires, you can choose one of our simple yearly plans to keep using the software. All your data, inventory records, and settings will remain safe and carry over directly. We never lock or delete your offline database.
Does the software support multiple computers/terminals?
CredaERP is optimized as a high-performance, machine-bound single-counter POS to ensure maximum reliability and speed. If you need multiple terminals billing at the same time, please reach out to us on WhatsApp to discuss custom local network server options.
How do I take backups of my sales data?
CredaERP automatically prompts you to save a local backup file on your computer or an external USB drive every time you exit the application. You can also connect it to your personal Google Drive or OneDrive folder for automatic backups.
Where can I download a statutory Schedule H1 drug register format?
You can download a free statutory Schedule H1 register template directly from our Free Templates Hub. Additionally, CredaRx automatically logs every Schedule H1 sale during billing so you never have to maintain manual paper registers for drug inspections.
Can I send the Windows download link to my WhatsApp from my phone?
Yes! If you are browsing from a smartphone, scroll to our Download section and tap "Send Download Link to WhatsApp". The Windows installer link and setup guide will be sent directly to your WhatsApp so you can download it on your counter computer whenever you're ready.
Is a drug license number/GSTIN mandatory to start?
No. You can start your 3-month trial instantly using just your business name. You can fill in your drug license number, GSTIN, and retail details later under the 'Store Settings' panel inside the application when you are ready.
Are software updates free?
Yes, all updates, feature enhancements, and bug fixes are 100% free. When a new version is released, you will see an update notification in the app, and you can download it in one click without losing any of your existing billing data.
